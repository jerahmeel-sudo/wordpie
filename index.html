Change one letter. Make a new word. Don't freeze.

Start from a word like HELL. Each player swaps exactly one letter to make a real word: HELL, BELL, BALL, BALD. Run out of time or give up and you're out. Last player standing wins.

How a turn works

  1. Pass the device to the player whose name is shown. They tap Start my turn and the clock begins.
  2. Everyone plays on their own phone. When it's your turn, your phone beeps and your clock starts.
  3. Type a word that changes exactly one letter of the current word. Same length, same positions.
  4. No repeats. A word already in the chain can't be played again.
  5. With the dictionary as referee, the clock pauses while it checks the word is real and shows everyone what it means. Not a word? The clock restarts and you try again.
  6. Stuck? Tap Give up. Out of time? You're out too. The next player keeps going from the same word.
  7. In a game of 4, players drop out one by one until a single winner is left.
